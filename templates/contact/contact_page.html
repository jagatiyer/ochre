Frequently Asked Questions

Where are Ochre Spirits made?

Every Ochre expression is imagined in Goa and crafted at a small batch distillery in South Goa where precision distillation and maceration led craftsmanship shape every bottle. Goa is not just our address. It is our philosophy.

What makes Ochre different from other Indian spirits brands?

Most Indian spirits brands look outward for validation. We don't. Ochre is built on the conviction that premium craft from India belongs at the world's finest tables — not as an alternative to imported spirits, but as a confident equal. Small batch, artisanal, and entirely unapologetic about it.

What is in the Ochre portfolio?

Ochre spans four categories today — Vodka, Rum, Gin, and Agave — with expressions ranging from Saffron Vodka and Nutty Berry Rum to Original Dry Gin, Plum Citron Gin, Original Agave Blanco, and Kiwi and Pear Agave. We also offer a non alcoholic cocktail premix line and a curated water range. Whiskey and Brandy expressions are currently in development and will be joining the portfolio soon. A House of Beverages with a great deal more still to come.

Which states is Ochre available in?

Ochre is currently available across Karnataka, Maharashtra, Goa, and Pondicherry, with active expansion into new markets underway. For the most current state availability and retail listings, reach us directly at ochrespirits.com/contact.

What is The Ochre Society?

The Ochre Society is our invitation only private dining circle — a curated hospitality membership where your dining experience at partner restaurants is hosted quietly by Ochre. No vouchers, no transactions at the table, no interruptions. Just presence. Entry is by invitation only.

Can I collaborate with Ochre for events, bartending, or brand partnerships?

Yes, and we are selective about it. Ochre partners with venues, bartenders, chefs, and cultural platforms that share our conviction around craft, quality, and modern celebration. If that sounds like you, we would like to hear from you at ochrespirits.com/contact.
