Redefining Craft Spirits

In the vibrant landscape of Goa, where sun-kissed beaches meet diverse communities, Ochre Spirits emerged with a bold mission - To redefine what craft spirits can be.

Founded in 2023, we quickly be game a talked-about craft spirit brand in Goa. But our vision extends far beyond local acclaim.

We are redefining what it means to create premium spirits…not about exclusivity. It is about creating a new standard where great spirits are celebrated.

Our Journey

2023

The Beginning

Ochre Spirits launches in Goa with our first collection of craft spirits, introducing Saffron Gin, Peach and Cherry Vodka, and our signature flavoured rums.

2024

Expansion

Introducing of new flavours including Rose & Raspberry Gin, Kiwi & Pear Gin and fruit-forward vodkas. Opening of India’s first-ever brand tasting room.

2025

Innovation continues

Launch of premium Saffron Vodkas and continued commitment to creating spirits that transcend boundaries and celebrate tradition with a modern twist.
